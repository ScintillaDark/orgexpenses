# Code of Conduct

We want participation in OrgExpenses to be respectful and constructive.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Treating people with respect and patience.
- Giving and receiving feedback constructively.
- Focusing discussion on the work, not on individuals.
- Respecting privacy and not publishing credentials or personal data.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, and intentionally disruptive behavior are
not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this
standard and may restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub.
